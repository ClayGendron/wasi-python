An `int` is 4-aligned on wasm32 by the C ABI, so the address is even in every future link, not only this one. musl's handles are `f<<16 | t<<1 | 1` (always odd), `(iconv_t)-1` (odd), or `malloc` pointers to a `stateful_cd`, which never alias a static object. No real handle can equal the sentinel.
