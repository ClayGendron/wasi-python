## Findings, most severe first

1. **On Windows the drafted comparison judges an intact tree changed, every call** (`context/specifications/128-…md`, Decision 2 second bullet and Decision 5; against `src/wasi_python/_zip.py:218-228` and `_artifacts.py:109-113`) — the record is written by `os.lstat` and the comparison would read `DirEntry.stat(follow_symlinks=False)`, and on Windows the two are not the same stat. READ, CPython 3.14 (`context/prior_art/cpython`, prior art read and cited, nothing copied): `Modules/posixmodule.c:16225-16226` builds a `DirEntry`'s lstat from the directory listing's `WIN32_FIND_DATAW` with `_Py_attribute_data_to_stat(&file_info, reparse_tag, NULL, NULL, …)`, after `find_data_to_file_info` (`:1998-2012`) has zeroed the `BY_HANDLE_FILE_INFORMATION` and copied only attributes, times and size; `Python/fileutils.c:1141` then gives `st_dev` the zeroed volume serial, `:1162-1165` gives `st_ino` the zeroed file index, and `posixmodule.c:16229-16230` sets `st_ctime` to the creation time; `os.lstat` on Windows goes through `win32_xstat_slow_impl` (`:2255`) with the id and basic info, so its `st_dev`, `st_ino` and `st_ctime` (the change time) are real. `_stamp` is `(st_dev, st_ino, st_size, st_mtime_ns, st_ctime_ns)`: three of its five fields differ, `seen != record` on every call, and the docs say the same ("On Windows, the st_ino, st_dev and st_nlink attributes of the stat_result are always set to zero"). The specification's sentence "on Windows `follow_symlinks=False` reads the reparse point as today's `os.lstat` does" is wrong, and "the same one lstat per entry the kernel sees today" holds on POSIX only (on Windows the `DirEntry` path makes no system call at all).
   Failure: on Windows, every `run` finds `_data_intact` false, `_zip_folder` removes and extracts matplotlib's data again (204 files written, read back and compared), and `test_the_data_is_extracted_and_recorded` fails in the own-runner shard's Windows leg. The guest still gets a correct tree, so the promise of spec 023 Decision 5 is not weakened; the specification's own purpose (less work per call) inverts on one of the three systems, and criterion 4 goes red.
   The fix, in exact terms, is the builder's: in Decision 2, `one os.lstat(entry.path) per DirEntry` in place of `one stat(follow_symlinks=False)` (the same one `lstat` the kernel sees today on every system, the same `_stamp` source as the record on every system; 0.89 ms against the prototype's 0.92 on macOS, EXECUTED, item 6); in Decision 1's first check, assert `os.lstat` was called exactly once per entry of the tree plus once for the root (on main: once per entry, once more per folder for `os.walk`'s link test, once for the root; on the fixture 7 against 10), `os.stat` once, `os.scandir` once per folder plus the root, and say that the lstat count alone tells the bodies apart; in Decision 5, drop the Windows sentence about `follow_symlinks=False` and say instead that the stamp comes from `os.lstat` on every system, as the record's does.
