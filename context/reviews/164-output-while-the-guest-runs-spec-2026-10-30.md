## Findings, most severe first

1. **Medium: `sys.unraisablehook` cannot be called as Decision 5 says** (Decision 5; Decision 1's row). The default hook refuses any argument but `UnraisableHookArgs`, a type Python code cannot name or build (EXECUTED on 3.10, 3.12 and 3.14). Failure: the builder calls `sys.unraisablehook(SimpleNamespace(...))`; the row passes, because it installs a recording hook that takes anything; in production the default hook raises `TypeError` inside the delivery wrapper, and the callback's exception is lost or, unwrapped, escapes into the home. Fix text for Decision 5: "An `Exception` from `on_output` is reported by `warnings.warn` as a `RuntimeWarning` naming the exception's type and message, outside every lock, as the runner reports other faults no caller can take; the callback is not called again in that call or turn." The row then checks one `RuntimeWarning` under `pytest.warns`. If the owner prefers the unraisable hook, the decision names a mechanism that works under the default hook, with a row that runs with the default hook installed.
2. **Medium: `_home.py` is not in Design, and as it stands it swallows the callback's exceptions** (`_home.py:95-121`, `_Box.run`; `:243-265`, `_Home.serve`; Decisions 2 and 5). A hand-off nobody waits on is a `_Box` whose `run` catches every `BaseException` into `box.error`, which no one reads. Under `run_async` the callback runs in an event loop callback, where asyncio hands an `Exception` to the loop's exception handler and lets `KeyboardInterrupt` and `SystemExit` out of `run_forever`. Failure: `test_a_base_exception_in_on_output_stops_the_call` raises nothing, since the `BaseException` went into an unread box; under `run_async` a `SystemExit` in the callback stops the embedder's loop instead of the call. Fix text for Decision 2 and Design: "`_home.py` gains `_Home.post(fn)`, a hand-off the guest thread does not wait for: `serve` runs it, and a `BaseException` that is not an `Exception` goes to `self.stop(e)`; `post` after a stop does nothing. Under `run_async` the home posts each batch with `loop.call_soon_threadsafe` to a wrapper that catches `Exception` (Finding 1) and hands any other `BaseException` to `home.stop`; a closed loop's `RuntimeError` at the post stops delivery for the call. The last delivery, after `capture.close()` or the turn's `settle()`, runs only when the home has not stopped." Add `_home.py` and a unit row for `post` to Design.
3. **Medium: an `OutputFeed` whose call never started never ends** (Decision 6). A task cancelled before its first step throws `CancelledError` into a coroutine that has run no line, so no `finally` in `run_async` runs (INFERRED from the coroutine protocol). Failure: `task = asyncio.create_task(run_async(argv, on_output=feed)); task.cancel(); async for chunk in feed: ...` waits forever; so does a feed handed to a coroutine that is never awaited. Fix text for Decision 6: "A feed is iterated with its task: `async for chunk in feed.follow(task)`, which ends once the task is done, whatever its outcome, after the chunks already queued. `run_async` also ends the feed in a `finally` that wraps its whole body, its argument checks included." Add `test_a_feed_ends_when_its_task_is_cancelled_before_it_starts`.
4. **Medium: three edited functions are past or at the line ceiling and unclaimed** (`tests/checks/test_rules.py:1461-1472`; Kind line). `_call.py:_instance` is listed at exactly 157 lines and gains the feed's build and the last delivery; `_sessions.py:_turn_inside` is 78 lines (EXECUTED) and gains the per-turn feed and delivery; `runner.py:_checked` is 76 and gains two checks. The list only shrinks, and none is ever added. Failure: `test_no_function_is_over_eighty_lines` is red at the first push. Fix text for Design: "The last delivery and the `Completed` of a call and of a turn are built by one helper in `_call.py` that `_instance` and `_turn_inside` both call; the feed is made in `_outputs._feed_for(on_output, keep, home, loop)`; the two keyword checks go to `_limits`. `_instance` falls by what it moves; `_turn_inside` and `_checked` stay at or under 80." 163 edits the same three functions; this specification should land first and do the extraction, so 163 extends the helper (163's review, Finding 4).
5. **Medium: Decision 13 predicts the growth instead of measuring it, and the prediction is likely wrong** (Decision 13; criterion 5). The bootstrap is marshalled into both binaries, and the plain binary unmarshals it at every start, so a larger `turn` adds instructions to every plain call; the snapshot's heap shifts with the bootstrap's size, which can move `id`-ordered work in any call (INFERRED). Failure: own and upstream rows of plain calls grow, the news file does not claim them, and the post-merge acceptance records them `unclaimed` (refused outright before 161 lands). Fix text for Decision 13: "Both binaries grow by the export, the branch in `turn` and the bootstrap's larger code; turns grow by the branch, and calls may grow by the bootstrap's size. 6. **Low: `turn_input`'s edges.** Fix text, Decision 9: "A second `turn_input` before `turn` frees the first buffer; a `NULL` pointer with a length above 0 is the host's out-of-memory, and the turn raises `MemoryError` before its code, as a `NULL` code buffer does; `shutdown`, `prepare` and 162's `checkpoint` free a held buffer. Fix text, Decision 11: "Under `--stream`, standard output holds the chunk lines and then the result line or the error line; Ctrl-C ends it at exit 130 after the chunks already written. `cli.py`'s docstring and `docs/reference/cli.md` say so."
11. **Low: what a streaming reader sees under the tail.** Decision 4 delivers each stream's first `output_bytes` while Decision 8 keeps its last. 12. **Low: the documents.** `mkdocs.yml`'s nav gains `how-to/output-while-it-runs.md`. `docs/how-to/sessions.md` says a turn's `stdin` is a reader over the bytes: `sys.stdin.fileno()` raises and descriptor 0 stays empty.
13. **Low: `runner.py`'s headroom.** It is 1,318 lines under a ceiling of 1,500 (EXECUTED); this specification adds three keywords and their docstrings to four entry points, and 162 and 163 add more. Fix text, Design: "the pull request states `runner.py`'s length after its change."
