The top-level env is held by K4 to `set_key.ENV`, so it stays as it is. Or set `UV_BUILD_CONSTRAINT: ""` on exactly those two steps, with rule 21 admitting only that. 2. **Medium: nothing runs the new path before the tag, and a failure there costs a version** (Decisions 1, 4; `docs/contributing/release-process.md`, "Publish" and "Signing").
